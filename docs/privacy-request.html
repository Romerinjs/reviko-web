Reviko Privacy Request

Use this page to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or another privacy right that may apply to your personal information.

Submit a Request

Email privacy@reviko.io and include:

  • your full name;
  • email address associated with Reviko;
  • the Merchant or loyalty program involved, if relevant;
  • the type of request;
  • enough information for us to reasonably identify the relevant account or data.

Reviko may request additional information to verify identity before fulfilling a request.

Available Request Types

Depending on applicable law, you may request:

  • access;
  • correction;
  • deletion;
  • portability;
  • information about disclosures;
  • withdrawal of certain consent;
  • opt-out rights that apply under state privacy law.

Response

Reviko will respond within the time required by applicable law.
