Delete Your Reviko Account

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Reviko users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

How to Request Deletion

You may submit a deletion request by:

  1. using the account deletion option available inside Reviko, when available; or
  2. emailing privacy@reviko.io from the email address associated with your account.

Include enough information for us to reasonably identify the account.

What Happens Next

Reviko may verify your identity before completing the request.

After verification, Reviko will delete or de-identify personal information that is no longer reasonably necessary, subject to information that must be retained for legal, accounting, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

Merchant Accounts

Deleting a Merchant account may affect active loyalty programs and customer passes. Outstanding Merchant obligations to Customers are not automatically eliminated by deletion of the Reviko account.
