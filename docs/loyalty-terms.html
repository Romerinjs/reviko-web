Reviko Standard Loyalty Program Terms

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Version: 2.0

These standard terms may be used as default customer-facing terms for loyalty programs powered by Reviko. A Merchant may publish additional or different program-specific terms.

1. Age Requirement

Participation through Reviko is intended only for individuals 18 years of age or older.

2. Voluntary Participation

Participation in a loyalty program is voluntary. By enrolling, the Customer agrees to these terms and any additional rules published by the participating Merchant.

3. Earning

Points, visits, stamps, or other loyalty credit are earned only through qualifying activity recognized as valid by the Merchant.

4. No Cash Value

Unless the Merchant expressly states otherwise, loyalty points, visits, stamps, and rewards:

  • do not constitute money or stored value;
  • may not be exchanged for cash;
  • do not accrue interest; and
  • are not transferable.

5. Rewards

Rewards are offered and provided directly by the participating Merchant. Rewards may be subject to product, location, timing, availability, expiration, or other restrictions disclosed by the Merchant.

6. Fraud and Abuse

The Merchant may cancel or reverse loyalty activity reasonably determined to result from fraud, duplication, manipulation, misuse, or abuse.

7. Program Changes

The Merchant may reasonably modify its loyalty program prospectively, subject to applicable law and any customer rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

8. Program Termination

A Merchant may terminate a loyalty program subject to applicable law and the Merchant’s customer-facing commitments. Previously earned rights or rewards may remain enforceable where required by law or the Merchant’s published terms.

9. Reviko's Role

Reviko is a technology platform operated by 77 Studio LLC. Reviko provides infrastructure used to administer the program.

The participating Merchant—not Reviko—is the provider responsible for the products, services, discounts, and rewards offered through the program.

10. Apple Wallet and Google Wallet

A loyalty pass may be available through Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. Those services are operated by third parties and are subject to their own terms and availability.

11. Privacy

Information related to participation in a loyalty program is handled in accordance with the Reviko Privacy Policy and, where applicable, the participating Merchant’s privacy notice.

12. Contact

Questions about a specific reward or program rule should first be directed to the participating Merchant.

Questions about Reviko technology may be directed to support@reviko.io.
